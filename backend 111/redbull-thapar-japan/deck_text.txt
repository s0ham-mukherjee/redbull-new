Attention isn't opportunity: we turn India signals into evidence-backed IP decisions
Brief: GEEK-PS-01, Predicting Indian Market Potential for Japanese Media Content Using Big Data. All capabilities shown are proposed. 1
PROBLEM
Signals about Japanese IP in India are scattered across search, 
video, wiki and retail. Spikes look like demand but often fade; 
licensors lack a disciplined way to decide what to investigate.
USER & DECISION
A licensing, distribution or merchandise lead at a Japanese 
studio or licensor, or their India partner, deciding which IPs 
deserve investigation, why, and what to test next.

WHY IT MATTERS
JETRO (2026): imports of Japanese IP goods into India are 
currently very small, partly because import procedures (BIS 
certification for some products, IEC, GST) are complex and 
information is scarce.
Implication: a candidate needs commercial-path evidence, 
not just attention.
DEMAND SIGNALS, NOT REVENUE
Hakuhodo India (Jul 2026, n=1,200, 8 cities): 61.1% prefer dubbed 
anime; 73.1% of them choose Hindi. Crunchyroll data via Variety 
(Mar 2026): India watch time up 3.5x in a year.
Google Trends is relative (0–100), not volume: one signal to 
corroborate.
